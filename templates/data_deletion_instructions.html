How to delete your data

What we store

If you created an account, DevChatbot-AI stores:

  • your username and email address,
  • a hashed password, if you signed up with email,
  • your Facebook or Google account ID and profile picture link, if you signed in with them.

Your chat messages are not stored.

Request deletion

Send an email to info@trihonor.com from the address of your account, with the subject “DevChatbot-AI data deletion”.

We delete your account and all the data listed above within 30 days and confirm by email.

Signed in with Facebook?

You can also remove the app's access to your Facebook account: in Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, select DevChatbot-AI and choose Remove. Then send us the email above so that we delete the data we hold.
